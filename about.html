ABOUT EVENTLENS

A clearer plan for every moment.

EventLens helps event photographers go in ready: know what matters, when it happens and who is covering it.

01 / SPOT

Find the moments

Turn event details into a clear sequence of arrivals, highlights, people and reactions worth photographing.

02 / PLAN

Share the coverage

Build a shot list and divide responsibilities so the photo team knows where to focus.

03 / CAPTURE

Keep the plan close

Use the live checklist during the event to track completed shots and stay on top of key coverage.

EventLens plans photography coverage. It does not create or edit image files.
